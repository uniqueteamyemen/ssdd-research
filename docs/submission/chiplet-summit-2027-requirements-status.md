## Current official information

The official summit site lists **Chiplet Summit 2027** at the Santa Clara Convention Center on **30 March–1 April 2027** and links to a “2027 Call for Presentations.” At the time of this check, that 2027 call page contains no visible form fields, deadline, abstract limit, category list, upload specification, or copyright-consent text.[^home][^cfp2027]

## Historical 2026 form: planning reference only

The 2026 call is useful as a preparation guide, but it is **not a substitute for 2027 requirements**. Its form required a presenter biography, affiliation, one topic category, a presentation title, an abstract of at most **3,768 characters**, and copyright/privacy consent. Its listed topic choices included **Technology Areas: High-Performance Computing (HPC)**, among other applications, ecosystem, packaging, standards, system-design, and technology-area categories.[^cfp2026]

## Submission controls

The final submission must list the human presenter(s), affiliation, and rights holder(s) supplied or approved by the owner. It must not state or imply that an AI system is an author, presenter, or research contributor. The abstract and any supporting material must retain the claim boundaries recorded in the Cherry evidence package.
